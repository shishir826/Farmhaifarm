Our Story

We put the farm back between the field and your fridge.

FarmFresh started as a way to shorten the distance between the people who grow food and the people who eat it. No warehouses, no middlemen, no produce sitting around for weeks — just what was picked, sent straight to your door.

Our Journey

FarmFresh began in 2021 with a single delivery van and a handful of farmers around Kathmandu Valley who were tired of watching brokers take most of the margin on produce they'd spent months growing.

We built a direct line instead: farmers list what's ready to harvest, we pick it up the same morning, and it reaches your door before it has time to lose its snap. No cold storage queues, no resale markups.

Today that same idea runs at a bigger scale, but the rule hasn't changed — if a farmer picked it this morning, you should be eating it by tomorrow.
