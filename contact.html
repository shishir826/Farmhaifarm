Before You Write In

Quick answers

How fast is delivery after I order?

Most orders placed before 2 PM go out the same day and arrive within 24 hours, since we pack straight from that morning's harvest.

What if something arrives damaged or missing?

Message us with your order number through the form above or call the support line — we'll sort a replacement or refund the same day.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Yes, as long as the order hasn't left for delivery yet. Reach out through live chat for the fastest turnaround.

I'm a farmer — how do I partner with FarmFresh?

Select "Becoming a partner farm" in the message form with your location and what you grow, and our farm relations team will follow up.
